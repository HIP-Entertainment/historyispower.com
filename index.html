About the studio

HistoryIsPower Games is a small independent studio. We build games around a simple idea: the people who understand how power was won, kept and lost are the ones who shape what comes next.

Our games turn that into play. You start small, make smart moves and watch something of your own grow.

We build in the open with our players. Features in Trillionaire Tycoon such as daily rewards and renaming your company started as ideas from our community.

Version 0.7.1 Trillionaire Tycoon

Every building now has its own drawing

The last 12 business upgrades got brand-new art, from Bistro Chains and a restaurant skyscraper to a movie-camera headquarters, Vegas Strips and Global Carriers.

Dark mode, crypto and a new Investments screen

Pick Auto, Light or Dark in Profile. Investments has a fresh look with Real Estate, Stocks and Crypto tabs, and there are 5 new made-up crypto coins to trade.

New businesses: Gas Stations and Mega-Conglomerates

Open Gas Stations and grow them into Travel Plazas and Fuel Networks. Plus 14 newly drawn buildings, including the Lake Cabin, Moon Base, Casinos, Hotels and Airlines.
