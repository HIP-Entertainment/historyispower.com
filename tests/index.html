PC Google Play on the web

  1. Join our tester group with your Google account. Join the Google Group
  2. Open the test page in your browser and click Become a tester. Join the test on the web
  3. Wait at least 15 minutes for Google's systems to recognize you as a tester so you have access to the game.
  4. Install the game from Google Play on your device (or on Google Play Games for PC, where available).

Android Phones and tablets

  1. Join our tester group with the Google account you use in the Play Store. Join the Google Group
  2. On your phone or tablet, open the game on Google Play and tap Install. Get it on Google Play

Just joined the group? Google Play can take a few minutes to let you in.

Apple iPhone and iPad

  1. Install Apple's free TestFlight app from the App Store. Get TestFlight
  2. On your iPhone or iPad, open our invite and tap Accept, then Install. Join on TestFlight
